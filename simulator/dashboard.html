How It Works

The simulator generates fake emergency incidents from a bank of 30 real-world scenarios and POSTs them to the AERS backend every N seconds.


Each incident goes through the full pipeline: ML triage → ambulance dispatch → hospital selection → case log.


Use "Manual Fire" to inject specific risk levels instantly during a demo.
